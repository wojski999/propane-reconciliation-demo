# Product

## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

The approved specification defines the reader: a propane distributor owner and
accounting/operations team reviewing morning exceptions and their evidence.
Success is an inspectable synthetic report with deterministic financial numbers.

## Stack and constraints

Python 3.12, SQLite and standalone HTML. No server, JavaScript, external assets,
real integrations or client data. English repository and report. The issue #8
brief explicitly requests a small accessible business report; no new product or
visual-brand assumptions are introduced by this implementation.

## Evidence

The approved specification and synthetic source contracts govern the content.
KPI worksheets, check findings, source freshness and immutable raw records supply
the report. No client testimonials, production claims or live narrative.
