# Source-system research for a synthetic propane reconciliation demo

Research date: 2026-11-04. Public, first-party sources only. No client accounts, credentials, exports, or personal data were accessed. This is evidence for a specification, not a production integration contract.

## Findings and boundaries

QBO publishes accounting entities and their references. Cargas publicly describes operational capabilities and a QBO accounting integration, but the reviewed material does not establish a public delivery/tank API schema or the client's entitlements. The demo should use QBO-shaped synthetic objects and an explicitly **demo-defined operational format inspired by documented workflows**, never call its invented columns a Cargas export or API response.

## QuickBooks Online Accounting API

### Identity, fields, and links

Keep company/realm, entity type, and `Id` together as the source key. An invoice ID and payment ID are different identities even if their strings match. Retain `SyncToken`, `MetaData.CreateTime`, and `MetaData.LastUpdatedTime` for version/freshness evidence. `SyncToken` supports optimistic concurrency; it is not a delivery-ticket ID. References use `value` to identify the related object. Intuit's official SDK guide explains the ID/reference pattern: [PHP SDK quickstart](https://intuit.github.io/QuickBooks-V3-PHP-SDK/quickstart.html). Application-side crosswalks to operational IDs require confirmation; matching names or amounts alone is insufficient.

The following are selected reconciliation fields, not complete schemas or assertions that every field is present in every response. US company settings, item types, API version, and multicurrency affect availability. Canonical API pages are linked alongside accessible official SDK supplements; the latter include a shared QBO/QBW data model and do not prove that every inherited SDK property is supported by QBO.

- **Invoice:** `Id`, `DocNumber`, `TxnDate`, `DueDate`, `CustomerRef`, `Line`, `TotalAmt`, `Balance`, `TxnTaxDetail`, `CurrencyRef`, and `ExchangeRate` where applicable. An invoice represents a sale receivable; its balance is different from the original sale total. Inspect each line's `Id`, `Amount`, `DetailType`, and, for sales-item lines, `SalesItemLineDetail.ItemRef`, `Qty`, `UnitPrice`, and `TaxCodeRef`. Preserve discount and subtotal lines rather than treating every line as fuel. Sources: [Invoice API](https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/api/accounting/all-entities/invoice), [official Invoice SDK model](https://static.developer.intuit.com/sdkdocs/qbv3doc/ipp-v3-java-devkit-javadoc/com/intuit/ipp/data/Invoice.html), [official sales-item line model](https://static.developer.intuit.com/sdkdocs/qbv3doc/ipp-v3-java-devkit-javadoc/com/intuit/ipp/data/SalesItemLineDetail.html).
- **SalesReceipt:** `Id`, `DocNumber`, `TxnDate`, `CustomerRef` when supplied, `Line`, `TotalAmt`, `TxnTaxDetail`, `PaymentMethodRef`, `PaymentRefNum`, and `DepositToAccountRef`. This combines a sale and payment received at the sale; do not require an additional Invoice/Payment pair or count it twice. Sales-item lines identify `ItemRef` and quantity/price as above. Sources: [SalesReceipt API](https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/api/accounting/all-entities/salesreceipt), [official SalesReceipt SDK model](https://static.developer.intuit.com/sdkdocs/qbv3doc/ipp-v3-java-devkit-javadoc/com/intuit/ipp/data/SalesReceipt.html).
- **Payment:** `Id`, `TxnDate`, `CustomerRef`, `TotalAmt`, `UnappliedAmt`, `PaymentRefNum`, `PaymentMethodRef`, `DepositToAccountRef`, and `Line[].Amount`. The critical relationship is **`Line[].LinkedTxn[]`**, containing `TxnId` and `TxnType`; follow these to the actual Invoice, CreditMemo, or other supported transaction. A payment may cover multiple invoices/credits, or be unapplied. Do not assume one payment equals one invoice or infer application from customer/date alone. `UnappliedAmt` identifies an unapplied amount; it does not alone prove an error or an overpayment. Source: [Payment API, including supported linked types](https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/api/accounting/all-entities/payment).
- **CreditMemo:** `Id`, `DocNumber`, `TxnDate`, `CustomerRef`, `Line`, `TotalAmt`, and `RemainingCredit` where returned. Product lines retain item/quantity/price evidence. A customer credit is different from cash returned; use Payment application links when reconciling credits against invoices. Sources: [CreditMemo API](https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/api/accounting/all-entities/creditmemo), [official CreditMemo SDK model](https://static.developer.intuit.com/sdkdocs/qbv3doc/ipp-v3-java-devkit-javadoc/com/intuit/ipp/data/CreditMemo.html).
- **RefundReceipt:** `Id`, `DocNumber`, `TxnDate`, `CustomerRef`, `Line`, `TotalAmt`, `PaymentMethodRef`, and `DepositToAccountRef`. This records a refund for previously sold goods/services. Keep refund evidence separate from credit issuance and payment application. Do not invent a universally populated link to an original sale: require the actual source linkage or a confirmed crosswalk. Sources: [RefundReceipt API](https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/api/accounting/all-entities/refundreceipt), [official RefundReceipt SDK model](https://static.developer.intuit.com/sdkdocs/qbv3doc/ipp-v3-java-devkit-javadoc/com/intuit/ipp/data/RefundReceipt.html).
- **JournalEntry:** `Id`, `DocNumber`, `TxnDate`, `CurrencyRef`, `Line[].Amount`, and `Line[].JournalEntryLineDetail` with `PostingType` (Debit/Credit), `AccountRef`, and `Entity` where present. Debits and credits balance; the documented `TotalAmt` is always zero, so it is not journal revenue. A/R lines require a customer and A/P lines a vendor. An aggregate GL posting cannot establish a delivery-level sale link without supporting detail. Sources: [JournalEntry API](https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/api/accounting/all-entities/journalentry), [official line model](https://static.developer.intuit.com/sdkdocs/qbv3doc/ipp-v3-java-devkit-javadoc/com/intuit/ipp/data/JournalEntryLineDetail.html).
- **Customer:** `Id`, `DisplayName`, `Active`, `ParentRef`, `Job`, `BillWithParent`, `Balance`, `BalanceWithJobs`, and `CurrencyRef` where applicable. The parent/subcustomer relationship matters when applying payments and interpreting balances. A synthetic demo needs only fictional identity labels, not real contact/address data. Sources: [Customer API](https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/api/accounting/all-entities/customer), [official Customer SDK model](https://static.developer.intuit.com/sdkdocs/qbv3doc/ipp-v3-java-devkit-javadoc/com/intuit/ipp/data/Customer.html).
- **Item:** `Id`, `Name`, `Sku`, `Active`, `Type`, `UnitPrice`, `IncomeAccountRef`, `ExpenseAccountRef`, and, for inventory items, `TrackQtyOnHand`, `QtyOnHand`, `InvStartDate`, `AssetAccountRef`. `ItemRef.value` on sales lines resolves to `Item.Id`. Item types distinguish inventory, service, noninventory, bundles, and categories; not every item has stock. A current item price is not proof of the authorized historical/contract price. QBO quantity is not automatically gallons or a tank reading. Sources: [Item API](https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/api/accounting/most-commonly-used/item), [official Item properties including Sku](https://static.developer.intuit.com/sdkdocs/qbv3doc/ippdotnetdevkitv3/html/850f7b3b-9e8b-679d-9389-6a487f859b39.htm).

**Design consequence:** retain source IDs and line/application links. Do not add invoice totals, payment receipts, and journals together as revenue; they can describe the same business event. A deposit reconciliation will additionally require QBO Deposit/account detail and POS settlement evidence; the eight entities above alone do not establish bank settlement.

### CDC, rate limits, and OAuth 2.0

CDC takes entity types and `changedSince`, returns full changed objects, and includes deletions in its lookback. Its window is the previous **30 days**, with at most **1,000 objects per response**; use smaller windows if approaching that cap. CDC is a synchronization tool, not an immutable full history or a replacement for initial loading and recovery after long gaps. Store a successful watermark only after processing the complete response and preserve deletion evidence. Sources: [canonical CDC guide](https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/learn/explore-the-quickbooks-online-api/change-data-capture), [Intuit Developer Group's accessible CDC article](https://medium.com/intuitdev/building-smarter-with-intuit-stay-in-sync-with-cdc-171f8094dd37). The latter explicitly lists unsupported types; verify the deployed entity set against current documentation before production.

The published REST limits are **500 requests/minute per realm**, **10 requests/second per realm and app**, and a **1,000-entity query response maximum** requiring pagination. Production HTTP 429 guidance says wait 60 seconds before retrying; requests exceeding 120 seconds time out. Batch recommendations/limits are separate. The current page also describes Builder-tier included usage of 500,000 CorePlus calls per workspace/month; this is separate from burst limits and is not a quote for the client's total costs. Check the app's actual partner tier and billing. Source: [Intuit limits and throttles](https://static.developer.intuit.com/output_html/qbo/docs/learn/limits-and-throttles.html).

Production access requires the owner's OAuth 2.0 authorization for the company/realm, registered redirect URI, authorization-code exchange, bearer access token, and accounting scope `com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting`. The official client describes 3,600-second access-token validity and refreshing using the latest returned refresh token. Persist rotated tokens securely and handle revocation/reauthorization as an import failure, never silently reuse stale data. Sources: [OAuth guide](https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/develop/authentication-and-authorization/oauth-2.0), [Intuit-owned OAuth client](https://github.com/intuit/oauth-jsclient). Intuit support describes 100-day refresh-token validity: [support response](https://help.developer.intuit.com/s/question/0D5TR00000xEGb60AG/refresh-token-it-says-expires-after-100-days-rolling-i-would-like-to-find-out-what-this-means-does-it-so-long-as-the-access-tokens-are-renewed-each-time-the-100-days-is-reset-so-it-never-expires-so-long-as-we-keep-getting-new-access-tokens). Use returned expiry information and current documentation rather than assuming perpetual access. The accounting scope is not a dedicated read-only scope: a read-only integration must enforce GET/query-only behavior and restricted credential handling in its implementation. No live authorization is needed for the synthetic demo.

## Cargas Energy: verified public capabilities versus unknown contracts

### Verified product claims

- Delivery Mobile advertises digital tickets, on-site printing, meter integration, inventory visibility, dispatching, and price updates. These support modeling a delivery event and meter-origin evidence, but do not reveal meter payload fields, temperature basis, or how corrections/voids are exported. [Cargas Real-Time Mobile](https://cargasenergy.com/real-time-mobile/).
- Tank monitoring displays readings with customer/tank relationships, links monitor IDs to tanks, and can create delivery tickets based on configured levels. This establishes operational relationships, not a public API's field names or a guaranteed sensor accuracy. [Cargas tank monitors](https://cargasenergy.com/tank-monitors/).
- Public support distinguishes FTP bulk-file imports from API monitor integrations such as Otodata. Import polling frequency does not control the vendor's measurement/file-update frequency; repeated imports can contain the same reading. [Tank Monitor Import Frequency](https://support.cargasenergy.com/hc/en-us/articles/35831915470989-Best-Practices-Tank-Monitor-Import-Frequency).
- Projected Gallons uses usage/projection data and can be blank; Projected Delivery Gallons can draw on monitor readings or averages, and Preset Gallons is the ticket's planned quantity. These are not interchangeable with metered delivered gallons. [Projected Gallons explanation](https://support.cargasenergy.com/hc/en-us/articles/47672740024077-How-does-the-Projected-Gallons-column-work-in-the-Delivery-Center).
- Cargas says it has its own APIs and prebuilt/partner integrations. That is a capability statement, not evidence of open self-service access or a public schema. [Official buyer's guide, integration section](https://cargasenergy.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Cargas-Energy-2022-Buyers-Guide.pdf).
- The QBO integration explicitly exports **GL distributions, payables, and deposits** to mapped accounting accounts. Setup involves Cargas/an accounting partner and credentials/account mappings. It does **not** establish that each delivery becomes a QBO Invoice or each customer payment a QBO Payment. Confirm the client's posting configuration before defining matching rules. [Cargas accounting integration](https://cargasenergy.com/cargas-energy-accounting-software/).
- The Sage Intacct integration separately documents GL exports, including interim month-to-date exports. Do not infer that its transport, granularity, or schedule is identical to the QBO integration. [Cargas–Sage Intacct integration PDF](https://cargasenergy.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/Cargas-Energy-Sage-Intacct-Integration.pdf).
- Cargas Query offers reusable lists/reports with configurable queries. The reviewed page does not establish a universal CSV export contract. [Cargas reporting](https://cargasenergy.com/software/reporting/). A Cargas-hosted general Sage Intacct reporting sheet advertises CSV/Excel exports, but that is **not proof of Cargas Energy operational CSV availability**: [Sage Intacct reporting sheet](https://cargas.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/Reporting-Dashboards-Data-Sheet.pdf).
- Cylinder Exchange advertises multiple exchange locations per customer, inventory adjustment from fills/loads/unloads, mobile invoice email and payment acceptance. Its POS app addresses over-the-counter propane/accessory sales. Neither page establishes the client's POS vendor or transaction schema. [Cylinder Exchange](https://cargasenergy.com/software/cylinder-exchange/), [Cargas POS](https://cargasenergy.com/point-of-sale/).

### Not established by these sources

No endpoint names, request/response schemas, database tables, CSV column layouts, authentication method for a customer integration, incremental/deletion contract, sandbox entitlement, API quota, or integration price was verified for Cargas delivery/customer/tank data. Do not interpret lack of public evidence as lack of the capability. API/report/CSV access, licensing and pricing require confirmation with the client and Cargas. The synthetic operational dataset must label its own ticket, tank, customer, meter, measurement timestamp, and correction references as **demo fields**.

## POS and cylinder exchange: first-party examples

The client POS is unknown. Square provides a documented example of a real POS data model, not a claim that the client uses Square:

- An order line has `uid`, `catalog_object_id`, `name`, decimal-string `quantity`, optional `quantity_unit`, `base_price_money`, tax/discount references, and calculated totals. Whole-number quantity without a unit means a count. Preserve units, tax inclusion, discounts, and line IDs. [Square OrderLineItem](https://developer.squareup.com/reference/square/objects/orderlineitem).
- Payment fields include `id`, `order_id`, `location_id`, timestamps, `status`, `source_type`, `amount_money`, `total_money`, `refunded_money`, `refund_ids`, `processing_fee`, and optional external `reference_id`. Money is represented in the currency's smallest denomination. Avoid importing card details. A completed sale, a refund, and an approved-but-incomplete payment differ. [Square Payment](https://developer.squareup.com/reference/square/objects/Payment).
- Settlement is separate from sales: payout entries carry payout identity and gross/fee/net amounts plus type-specific source details. A bank deposit may combine sales, fees, refunds, and timing differences; gross daily POS sales should not be compared directly to a net deposit. [Square PayoutEntry](https://developer.squareup.com/reference/square/objects/PayoutEntry).

Blue Rhino distinguishes an exchange from purchase without an empty return and labels its product as 15 lb net propane. This is a product example, not a universal cylinder fill specification. [Blue Rhino product](https://delivery.bluerhino.com/products/blue-rhino-propane). Paladin POS documents separate new-tank and exchange SKUs with a shared inventory SKU and shows full/empty cage counts diverging. [Paladin's Blue Rhino setup](https://portal.paladinpos.com/knowledge-base/ace-hardware-blue-rhino-propane-exchange-program-setup/).

**Demo implication:** distinguish refill gallons, full-cylinder sales, exchanges, empty returns, and cylinder-container inventory. A cylinder count is not a gallon measurement; do not assume all “20 lb” cylinders contain 20 lb or convert counts using a guessed constant. Any synthetic conversion must state a demo-only fill mass and approved conversion basis. Confirm tax, deposit/container-charge, void/return, split-tender, and settlement rules.

## Propane physical inventory and measurement

NIST Handbook 44 (2026), §3.32, applies to stationary and vehicle-mounted LPG liquid-measuring devices; mass meters are covered separately. Section UR.2.6 requires recorded sales representations from vehicle meters. Sections S.4.4 and UR.2.4 address temperature compensation, including the 60 °F reference and labeling on applicable invoices. The US gallon is 231 cubic inches. These are measurement standards, not a statement of the client's Texas legal obligations. [NIST Handbook 44, 2026](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/hb/2026/NIST.HB.44-2026.pdf), [current-edition index](https://www.nist.gov/pml/owm/nist-handbook-44-current-edition).

**Design inference:** retain observed-volume gallons separately from gallons corrected to 60 °F, their basis, and measurement time. Never reconcile mixed bases or guess a correction from temperature alone. Obtain the meter's documented correction method and supporting product/density data. Unknown basis or uncertain/stale sensor evidence should yield “insufficient evidence,” not a loss verdict.

A propane-industry operations handbook describes bulk-plant loading and unloading of bobtails/transports, rail receipts, and cylinder filling: [NCPCM Operations & Maintenance Handbook, §5](https://www.ncpcm.org/common/Uploaded%20files/Tech%20Support/om%20handbook.pdf). This supports separate physical locations and movement directions; it does not prescribe a universal inventory CSV.

**Demo-defined evidence to retain:** bulk-plant tank identity and timestamped opening/closing readings; capacity/calibration basis and reading quality; incoming transport receipt/load ticket; bobtail load/return transfer ticket; customer delivery meter ticket; product, quantity, unit and temperature basis; origin/destination; correction/void references. These are proposed normalized records, not Cargas columns or a claim that all records are client-accessible.

**Accounting inference:** reconcile each location using opening stock + receipts + transfers in − transfers out − external sales/deliveries ± documented adjustments against closing evidence. A plant-to-bobtail load is an internal transfer, not a second sale or a company-wide loss. Avoid double subtraction when a POS sale and delivery ticket describe the same movement. Percent-full readings require validated tank calibration/capacity and uncertainty; they are not inherently precise gallons. Match cutoff times and record late arrivals. Tolerances require client approval and measurement evidence; NIST device tolerances are not automatically daily inventory-loss allowances.

## Unknowns to confirm with the client

1. Which systems and versions are actually deployed, including POS, Cargas modules, meters, and monitors? Who owns the accounts and can authorize read-only access?
2. Which system owns customer A/R, invoices, credits, tax, and price history? Does QBO receive detailed sales or summary GL? How are payables/deposits posted, at what cadence, and with which supporting IDs?
3. Can Cargas provide documented API access or scheduled exports for tickets, actual metered quantities, customers/tanks, readings, inventory movements, corrections and voids? Obtain redacted schemas, export samples, licensing/pricing, quotas, retention, support and sandbox terms privately before implementation.
4. Are CSV/report exports available for those exact records, with stable IDs and machine-readable units? Can extraction detect partial files, deleted records, revised tickets, duplicate batches and missing intervals?
5. What are the cross-system keys and cardinalities? One ticket may span invoices or aggregate postings; anonymous POS sales may not have customer IDs. What is the approved fallback when no deterministic link exists?
6. What is the authorized effective-dated price/contract list, tax treatment, discount/credit approval policy and refund linkage? A catalog price discrepancy alone is not misconduct.
7. What are the POS settlement batch, bank-deposit, fee, refund, cash-drawer, split-tender and cutoff rules? Is QBO Deposit/account detail accessible?
8. What quantity units and volume bases apply to each meter, supplier ticket, truck and tank? Obtain calibration tables, correction methods and sensor quality/freshness metadata. Which readings are projections versus observations?
9. How are truck returns, plant transfers, cylinder fills/exchanges/empty returns and adjustments recorded? Who approves tolerances and the “no verdict” policy?
10. What operational timezone/business-day cutoff applies? Confirm El Paso site time rather than using the developer/server timezone. What latency is acceptable before stale-data alerts? Who receives import-failure alerts?
11. Confirm app partner-tier costs, QBO OAuth consent/reconnection responsibilities, least-privilege controls, and retention/security requirements. No real credentials or client records belong in the public demo.

## Source access and limitations

Official pages were consulted on 2026-11-04 through web search/open, plus a direct HTTP attempt. Several Intuit canonical SPA pages returned only “Compiling and pre-filling your Intuit info”; accessible official SDK pages, the Intuit-owned OAuth client and Intuit Developer Group's CDC article supplied corroboration. SDK pages are older and share QBO/QBW types, so production field support still needs current API documentation and sandbox verification. Static mirror attempts for CDC/OAuth/entity pages failed. Cargas pages were readable through the web tool; direct Python HTTP fetching of the reporting page returned 403. No login, paid portal, client export or gated integration guide was used. Public marketing claims establish advertised behavior, not deployed configuration, accuracy, SLA, price or a data-access contract.
